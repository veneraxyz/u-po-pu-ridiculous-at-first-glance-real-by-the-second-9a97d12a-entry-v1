One sentence pitching a bizarre startup that sounds absurd until the business logic clicks: renting calm, vest-wearing emotional-support chickens to stressed office workers by the hour.

The joke lands first — a hen in a vest on your lap — then the nod: stressed workers will pay $30 an hour for anxiety relief that actually works, faster than any meditation app.

## How it works

`main.ts` (TypeScript, Node 22, stdlib only) writes the pitch to `entry.txt` in the directory given by `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (falling back to `/output`). It enforces the competition gate before writing — exactly one line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace — then reads the file back and verifies it is byte-identical to the sentence plus a single trailing newline.

## The pitch

> We rent calm, vest-wearing emotional-support chickens to stressed office workers for $30 an hour, because a warm hen on your lap lowers anxiety faster than any meditation app ever will.
